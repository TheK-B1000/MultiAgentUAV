\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\paragraph{Evaluation.}
Each team size plays two scripted opponent regimes, Pole~A and Pole~B, that demand different
team strategies. For strategies $(\pi_A, \pi_B)$ we report the crossover
$\Delta_A = V(\pi_A, A) - V(\pi_B, A)$ and $\Delta_B = V(\pi_B, B) - V(\pi_A, B)$, where $V$ is the
win rate; both are positive when each strategy wins more on its own pole. Strategy-conditioned
students play $\pi_A$ and $\pi_B$ with their strategy input fixed to $z_0$ and $z_1$. All cells
of a table share the same evaluation seeds and deterministic actions; we report means with 95\%
paired bootstrap intervals over seeds.

\subsection{$2$v$2$}
\label{sec:exp-2v2}

\paragraph{Our system.}
Two specialists; on Pole~A the attacker is paired with a learned defender under a fixed role
allocator (the agent nearest the home flag defends, fixed for the episode).
On $\TwoSepN$ fresh seeds its crossover is
$\Delta_A = \TwoSepDA$ {\small$[\TwoSepDALo, \TwoSepDAHi]$} and
$\Delta_B = \TwoSepDB$ {\small$[\TwoSepDBLo, \TwoSepDBHi]$}.
