\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:exp-setup}

Each team size is evaluated against two scripted opponent regimes, Pole~A and Pole~B,
certified in advance to demand different team strategies. For a pair of strategies
$(\pi_A, \pi_B)$ we measure the win rate $V(\pi, \cdot)$ of each on each pole and report the
\emph{crossover}
\begin{equation}
  \Delta_A = V(\pi_A, A) - V(\pi_B, A), \qquad
  \Delta_B = V(\pi_B, B) - V(\pi_A, B),
\end{equation}
so that both are positive when each strategy is the better one on its own pole. For a
strategy-conditioned student, $\pi_A$ and $\pi_B$ are the student with its strategy input
fixed to $z_0$ and $z_1$. Every cell is played on the same evaluation seeds with
deterministic actions; a fresh environment is built from each seed. We report mean effects,
with 95\% paired percentile bootstrap intervals over seeds ($20{,}000$ resamples).

\subsection{Methods}
\label{sec:exp-methods}

\paragraph{Separated (ours).}
Two independent specialists. On Pole~A, the attacking specialist is combined with a learned
defender policy $\pi_D$ under a fixed role allocator (CLOSEST\_DEFENDS: the $k$ agents nearest
the home flag defend, fixed for the episode; $k=1$ at $2$v$2$).

\paragraph{Specialists without roles.}
The same two specialists before the attack/defend split, with no allocator.

\paragraph{Sharing baselines.}
All three are distilled from the same two teachers ($\pi_A$, $\pi_B$) on the same frozen set of
teacher states, collected under the same allocator, with the same objective
$\tfrac12\mathrm{KL}(\pi_A\,\|\,\pi(\cdot\mid o,z_0)) + \tfrac12\mathrm{KL}(\pi_B\,\|\,\pi(\cdot\mid o,z_1))$
and the same budget (20 epochs, batch 256, Adam at $3\times10^{-4}$, gradient clipping at 1.0,
no weight decay). Only the sharing structure differs.
\emph{Share-Encoder} shares the convolutional observation encoder and keeps a private
body and action heads per strategy.
\emph{Fully Shared+$z$} is one network that receives the strategy as a learned 16-dimensional
embedding.
\emph{Generalist} is the same single network with no strategy input; with nothing to
condition on, it fits the average of the two teachers.

\paragraph{Role allocation.}
To isolate the allocator, we evaluated the separated system and the specialists without roles
on the same $\TwoSepPairedN$ seeds. With the allocator, the crossover is
$\Delta_A = \TwoSepPairedDA$, $\Delta_B = \TwoSepPairedDB$; without it,
$\Delta_A = \TwoSpecDA$, $\Delta_B = \TwoSpecDB$.
